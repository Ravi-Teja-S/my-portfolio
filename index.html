01Jenkins High Availability on Kubernetes
HPE · MAR 2026–PRESENT

Leader election runs on a Kubernetes Lease object, state lives on a shared PVC gated by an is_leader file, and every Helm upgrade deliberately flips leadership — so failover isn't just designed, it's proven on every deploy.
